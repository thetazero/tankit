# Design direction

**Rugged, solid metal, clear exposed machinery.**

Inspired by the look of the tech in Villeneuve's *Dune* films: the feel of the
machines, not the setting, story or any specific designs.

- **Solid metal:** thick plates and heavy, chunky volumes in weathered gunmetal.
  Nothing hollow, plastic or shiny; nothing glows except combat effects.
- **Rugged:** worn, dusty, scratched, built to take abuse. Blunt, faceted
  shapes over sleek curves.
- **Exposed machinery:** you can see how things move (pistons, actuators,
  joints, gearing, suspension), and they actually move when the machine does.

Bright colour is reserved for combat (shots, hits, shields, damage) so the
action stays readable against the muted machines.
