# Design direction

**Rugged, solid metal, clear exposed machinery.**

Inspired by the look of the tech in Villeneuve's *Dune* films: the feel of the
machines, not the setting, story or any specific designs.

- **Solid metal:** thick plates and heavy, chunky volumes in weathered gunmetal.
  Nothing hollow or plastic.
- **Rugged:** worn, dusty, scratched, built to take abuse. Blunt, faceted
  shapes over sleek curves.
- **Exposed machinery:** you can see how things move (pistons, actuators,
  joints, gearing, suspension), and they actually move when the machine does.

**Glow where something is running.** Light comes from what powers a thing:
coil slots on a rail cannon, a laser's lens, crystal that's been woken, and
everything the Ithessar make (their machines are plasma held in crystal, so
the glow is the machine working: spines, vents, emitters, seams up their
monoliths). Shots, hits and blasts glow brightest, so a fight still reads at a
glance.

The Ithessar are the exception to the rest: knapped dark crystal instead of
plates, and parts held in place by the field instead of bolted on.
